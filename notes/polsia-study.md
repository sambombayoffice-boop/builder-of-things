# Polsia — study notes (pitch & "made by AI" stance)

Reference for Builder of Things / Bombaybot positioning. Polsia is an AI platform
that claims to build and run whole companies autonomously ("AI that runs your
company while you sleep"). Raised ~$30M, ~$250M valuation. It's both admired and
heavily criticised. We're studying the **craft of the pitch**, not copying the company.

## What we like (worth borrowing)

- **One-line wedge that sells a feeling, not a feature.** "Runs your company while
  you sleep" promises *outcome + relief*, not a tool. Our equivalent should sell the
  feeling (a studio/OS that builds *for* you) over a feature list.
- **Unapologetic "made by AI" stance.** They lead with the fact that AI did the work
  instead of hiding it. For an AI-first studio that's our identity too — own it as a
  badge of craft, not a disclaimer. The work being AI-made is the *point*, the proof.
- **Show the output, instantly.** Every generated company ships live on a real
  subdomain — "it made something real" in one glance. Our portfolio should make the
  AI-made artifact (film, site, product) tangible and immediate, not described.
- **Narrative > spec sheet.** They raised on story and momentum. A studio's pitch is
  a film, not a feature table. Keep the site cinematic and confident.

## What to avoid (their cautionary tale)

- **Don't optimise "launches" over "lives".** ~94% of their companies were abandoned;
  ~48% monthly churn. Volume looked great, retention was the wound. Our proof should be
  *things that keep running / get watched / get used*, not a count of things spun up.
- **Validate before building.** Nearly every 1-star review traced to building before
  checking anyone wanted it. A "should this exist?" beat belongs before the make.
- **Don't inflate the numbers.** Their ARR (folding in one-off sales + customers' ad
  spend, then ×12) became the headline *against* them. Credibility is the moat — claim
  only what's real.
- **Security is first-class, not a footnote.** They were reported leaking customer
  secrets; it gutted trust. Anything of ours that holds keys/credentials treats secret
  handling as a feature, not an afterthought.
- **Keep — and admit — the human in the loop.** They got dinged for implying full
  autonomy while humans reviewed outputs. "AI does the work, you stay in command" is the
  more honest and more defensible pitch.

## One-line takeaway

Borrow Polsia's **confidence and its "made by AI is the point" framing**; reject its
**inflated metrics and launch-count vanity**. Our differentiator: AI-made work that is
real, lasts, and is honest about how it was made.
